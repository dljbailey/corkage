# Research: 27 new southwest London restaurant candidates

## 1. Summary and scope

**27 candidates: Clapham 7; Fulham 7; Wimbledon 7; Barnes 6.** This is a research shortlist, not a list of 27 restaurants permitting BYO and not permission to publish. Two restaurants have directly retrieved official public corkage policies: **MAAI by Nikita (£30/bottle, restrictions)** and **Constance (£35 still / £40 sparkling)**. Four others have retrieved third-party BYO evidence, of varying age and reliability.

**Website access date throughout: 2026-09-13.** This is NOT a restaurant confirmation date. No restaurant or merchant was contacted; no booking or form was submitted. Live websites, menus and opening hours are trading indicators, not same-day operational confirmation. Date-stamped evidence is identified below; undated pages remain undated.

The 200 names in `/tmp/corkage-sw-exclusions.json` were read and checked before selection. None of those venues, their obvious renamed versions, or another Trinity room is counted. No restaurant in Richmond, Putney, Battersea, Balham or Wandsworth has been silently substituted. South Wimbledon and Wimbledon Park are explicitly identified within the Wimbledon bucket. Wider-area **wine shops**, where necessary, are labelled separately.

**Definitions:** `not-published` means no policy located in the successfully retrieved pages specified for that record, NOT an exhaustive proof of absence and never permission. `inaccessible` means a material policy source could not be read; some other parts of the website may work. `third-party-only` requires a retrieved third-party passage, not merely a search summary. Third-party search snippets are separately marked leads.

**U = unknown / not established.** The policy fields are **fee; bottle size; bottle limits; applicable days; advance notice; public-versus-event scope**. Where a record says **all six U**, every one of these fields is unknown; do not insert a default 750ml allowance or assume public BYO. “Policy passage: none” means no citable passage was found, not an invented negative policy.

### Selection judgement

Strongest food/wine-fit priorities: MAAI, Knife, Constance, Manuka Kitchen, Brook House, Takahashi, The Black Lamb, Riva, Home SW13 and The Waterman’s Arms. The rest widen neighbourhood/cuisine coverage. **Pappa Ciccia, Brown Cow, Cher Thai, Bombay Delight, Dalchini and Elā & Dhanī are softer premium-fit choices**: worthwhile neighbourhood leads, not claimed equivalents of Noble Rot or Maison François. Elā & Dhanī also needs official trading verification. Quality was not stretched to 30 with generic chains, cafés, duplicate rooms or adjacent-area restaurants.
