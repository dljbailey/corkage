These are candidates, **not 50 confirmed BYO restaurants**, and the incomplete records must not be published as verified directory entries.

**Website accessed: 2026-09-13 (Europe/London).** This is not restaurant-personally-confirmed information. No restaurants or merchants were contacted; no booking forms, purchases, communications, repository edits or Git actions were performed. Prior lane report was read for merchant leads; material merchant claims were rechecked rather than copied blindly. Initial findings were checkpointed in this artifact before this expanded report.

### Evidence and scope rules
- **F** = actually fetched/read official page, raw HTML, image or extracted PDF. **S** = search-result evidence only, even if it points to an official page. **TP** = third party; publication date stated when established.
- **official-confirmed** means the particular provision quoted is official, within its stated scope. It does not mean unlimited BYO, all days, all rooms, or a restaurant telephone confirmation.
- **not-published** means no policy located in the successfully checked material, **not permission or refusal**. **inaccessible** means no sufficiently usable policy-bearing page was obtained; retrieval failure is never labelled policy absence.
- **For every not-published/inaccessible record, fee, wine categories, bottle-size limits, quantity limits, applicable days, list exclusions, service treatment and public/private scope remain UNKNOWN unless specifically stated.** A restaurant wine list does not imply either permission or refusal.
- Official booking-platform BYO/corkage amenity tags are retained only as low-confidence leads, not fee or policy proof.
- Pairings below are **editorial wine STYLE suggestions**, not the restaurants' recommendations or claims of merchant stock. Broad-cuisine pairings are explicitly conditional on the actual menu. Dated menus are evidence of style, not guaranteed future dishes.
- Merchant selections are ordered by a practical combination of geography and quality, not measured walking distance. **W = wider-travel alternative**, not nearby. Every record maps to three branch IDs; this is not a claim that three close-by shops were found. Retail availability and hours should be checked before travel.
