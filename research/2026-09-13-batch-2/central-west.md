# Research: Corkage Club batch two — 50 additional central and west London candidates

## Summary
Fifty additional restaurant candidates, excluding the supplied first-100 roster and renamed versions, are retained below. **Only four have affirmative corkage policies fetched from official websites**: Noizé, The Ninth, Cornus and A.Wong. These are candidates for the directory, not 50 confirmed BYO restaurants; most need direct permission before anyone brings wine.

**Website accessed: 2026-09-13, Europe/London.** This is not a restaurant-personally-confirmed date. No restaurants or merchants were contacted; no forms, purchases, publishing, source/repository edits or Git operations were undertaken. Only this research artifact was written. The supplied exclusions and earlier central/west merchant ledger were read before discovery. Other-lane restaurants and additional Hawksmoor/Noble Rot branches were excluded.

## Evidence and scope rules
- `official-confirmed`: affirmative policy actually fetched, including public HTML missed by readable extraction. `official-no`: explicit refusal. `third-party-only`: a BYO lead exists, but no current fetched official policy. `not-published`: no policy found **in the specified accessible pages checked**, not an exhaustive claim about the website. `inaccessible`: the principal official source could not be adequately retrieved; any third-party lead remains separately labelled.
- **Unknown is not permission, and unknown fee is not free.** Unless explicitly stated, bottle sizes, caps, sparkling/sake treatment, days, advance notice, service charge on corkage and private-event applicability are unknown. No policy is extended between restaurants, even where ownership is shared.
- **F** means fetched official page/HTML/PDF. **S** means search-result evidence only, not fetched proof. **T** means third-party source. Where a quotation concerns food or contact, it does not establish corkage.
- Current trading evidence means a live official restaurant/reservation/menu presentation or a current independent listing, not an in-person operational check. Six principal websites remain inaccessible; those six candidates have weaker trading verification. None of the retained venues had closure evidence located, but they are not all independently proved trading on the access date.
- Pairings are **editorial wine styles**, grounded in sourced cuisine or menu. Conditional examples describe how to choose from that cuisine, not invented current dishes. They are not restaurant advice, guaranteed merchant stock, or permission to BYO.
- Merchant triples are ranked editorially by geographic practicality plus specialist quality, not measured walking distance. Each ID refers to a specific branch below. “Wider travel” explicitly means not a nearby-shop claim.
- This is a researched shortlist, not a recommendation to publish all 50 unchanged. Ffiona’s and Pentolina are the more informal neighbourhood end of the premium spectrum; the rest range from wine-led destination bistros to grand hotel dining rooms. No café-only listing is counted. Toklas means the restaurant, not its bakery; ROVI is a distinct restaurant, not an Ottolenghi deli branch.
